Around the island

Tap a destination or move with the joystick, arrow keys or WASD. Drag to look around. Wade in up to your knees to swim. Ride the scooter into the sea and it returns to its parking spot. Steer with left or right. Climb the rock or a palm with up and down. Step up into the warung from its east side to stand behind the counter.

Space jumps on land, pumps the surfboard or swings a padel racket. Q waves and E interacts. Tap any portrait above the island to meet that resident.
